📜 The Official Pixel Rules

We maintain an open-minded, high-vibe atmosphere. Ensure you abide by these guidelines inside Pixel Hub!

01

Be Respectful

Treat all members with absolute kindness. Insults, bullying, and toxicity are strictly not tolerated. Keep it friendly and chill!

02

English Only

To keep the community connected internationally, all public text and voice channels must remain English. Dutch inside-jokes are occasionally allowed, keep it cozy!

03

No Spam or Advertising

Do not spam text channels, emojis, or mass-tag roles. No unsolicited advertisements, server link promotions, or DM-advertising.

04

Follow Admin Guidelines

Our Co-Admins are here to safeguard the vibe. If an Admin or Moderator instructs you to alter behavior, comply graciously.

05

No NSFW Content

Keep all graphics, profiles, names, and general conversations safe-for-work. Violations will lead to an immediate ban by Dyno.

06

Keep It Gezellig!

At the end of the day, it's all about having a great time. Share gaming memes, show your project builds, and chat with cool folks!
